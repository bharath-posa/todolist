# User guide

## Accounts
Create an account from **Create an account** on the sign-in page. Everything you add (tasks, projects,
metrics, points) is private to your account.

## Tasks
Type in the **Add a task…** box at the top of the task list and press Enter: the task is added, due
today, with priority 2. **More options** (or the round **＋** button on a phone's task list, which jumps
to the box) opens the full form, keeping what you typed:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Task | What needs doing |
| Project | Optional group (see Projects) |
| Starts / Due | When you plan to start and when it's due. The due date can't be before the start |
| Priority | Eisenhower-style: *not urgent/not important* (1) up to *urgent/important* (4) |
| Repeats | Does not repeat, every day, week, month or year |
| Tags | Comma-separated labels, e.g. `home, health` |
| Pomodoros planned / done | How many 25-minute focus sessions you expect, and how many you've done |
| Mark done when all planned pomodoros are finished | Finishes the task automatically after the last planned pomodoro |

Each task shows its title and one grey line with its project (with the project's colour dot), when it's
due or how often it repeats, and its tags. The colour of the round checkbox is the priority: grey (1),
blue (2), orange (3), red (4). Overdue tasks say so in red. Tap the line to open the task for dates,
pomodoros and **Timer**, **Edit** and **Delete**. Tick the circle to mark a task done; **Undo** in the message
at the bottom takes it back.

On the task form, **Today**, **Tomorrow** and **Next week** set the due day and keep the time. The start time
and pomodoro settings are under **More options**.

### Tabs
- **Today** (default): unfinished tasks due today or overdue, plus repeating tasks you haven't done yet
  this period. Tasks whose due day has passed are listed first under **Overdue**.
- **Repeating**: all repeating tasks.
- **One-off**: unfinished tasks that don't repeat.
- **Done**: finished tasks.

### Filters
The search box is always shown. **Filter** opens project, priority and tag; each one you set appears as a
chip under the search box (tap the chip to remove it). They all combine and the lists update straight away.

## Getting around
On a phone, Tasks, Projects, Metrics and Insights are in the bar at the bottom of the screen. On a tablet
they are in the top bar. On a wide screen a sidebar lists Tasks (with how many are due today), Metrics,
Insights and each project with its open tasks; **＋** next to Projects starts a new one.

Your initial at the top right opens the account menu: **Appearance** (Auto follows your device's light or
dark setting; Light and Dark fix it on this device) and **Sign out**.

### Repeating tasks
A repeating task starts over (not done, 0 pomodoros) when its period rolls over: the next day, the next
calendar week (Monday), the next month or the next year. This happens the next time you open the task list.

## Pomodoro timer
**⏲️ Timer** on a task opens a 25-minute focus timer. Each completed pomodoro is counted on the task.
When time is up an alarm sounds and a **Time's up** banner shows; **Stop** silences it. With notifications on
(see below) you also get a **⏰ Pomodoro done** or **⏰ Break over** notification, even if the screen is locked
or you switched apps. **Pause**, **Stop**, **Reset** or picking another timer cancels it. On iPhone the page's
own alarm is muted when the side switch is on silent; the notification follows your notification settings.

## Projects
**Projects** lists your projects with a colour dot and a progress bar (finished / all tasks). Tap a project
to see its tasks. The **⋯** menu on each project adds a task to it, edits it (including archiving, which
hides it from the task form) or deletes it.
Deleting a project keeps its tasks; they just have no project afterwards.

## Metrics
**Metrics** tracks any value over time, such as body weight or hours slept. Create a metric with a name and
unit, then add entries (value, time, optional note). Each metric shows its latest value, the change since
the first entry, the lowest and highest values, and a chart.

## Notifications
Your initial → **🔔 Notifications**.

- **Due reminders** for one-off tasks: when they're due, or 15 minutes, 1 hour or 1 day before.
- **Pomodoro timer alarm** when a running timer ends (see above).
- **Morning summary** at the time you pick (in your device's time zone): how many tasks are due today and
  overdue, with the first few names. Days with nothing to do are skipped.

Turn notifications on separately on each device. **On iPhone and iPad** (iOS 16.4 or later) notifications
only work in the Home Screen app: in Safari tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, open **Todo** from the Home
Screen, sign in, then turn notifications on. **Send a test** checks everything works. Devices are listed on the
same page and can be removed there.

## Insights, points and levels
**Insights** shows your activity for the last 30 days (tasks finished and pomodoros per day), finished vs.
open tasks by priority, and project progress.

- Finishing a task earns **5 × its priority** points (5–20). Each pomodoro earns **2**.
- Un-ticking a finished task takes its points back.
- Every **100 points** is a new level.
- Your **streak** counts consecutive days with at least one finished task or pomodoro. Yesterday still
  counts, so a streak isn't lost before you've done anything today.
- **Badges** unlock for milestones such as your first task, 10 and 100 tasks, 50 pomodoros, and 3-, 7- and
  30-day streaks.

Your level, points and streak are always shown in the top bar.
